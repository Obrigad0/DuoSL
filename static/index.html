DuoSL – Sign Language Practice

What's DuoSL

DuoSL is an app for practicing sign language interactively: step-by-step guided lessons or free training in front of the webcam, with real-time sign recognition.

Under the hood, DuoSL leverages state-of-the-art LLM-based models, specifically trained to recognize hand gestures and signs in real time, turning your webcam into a smart, interactive sign language tutor.
